## What the loader reads

`loaders.load_psketch_craft`: the primitives `wood`, `grass` and `iron` are
gathered; `workshop0`, `workshop1` and `workshop2` are facilities; every recipe
needs its ingredients and its workshop (`bed <- plank + grass + workshop1`).
`gold <- bridge + src:gold` and `gem <- axe + src:gem` come from the barrier
rules, with the treasure locations as base facts. `loaders.psketch_goals()`
returns the ten tasks of `hints.yaml` (make plank, stick, cloth, rope, bridge,
bed, axe, shears; get gold, gem); `hints.yaml` itself holds the policy
sketches (sequences of subtask names) that the paper gives its agents.

Changes: quantities are metadata only; in the source, using a workshop crafts
every recipe of that workshop whose ingredients are held, and here each recipe
is a separate action.
